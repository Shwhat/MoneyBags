# Limits

This page lists what MoneyBags does not do in this version.

- Two people per book, and one book per server.
- Splits are 50/50 (Y) or all for the other person (N). Other shares, such as 60/40, are planned.
- One person edits at a time. The other sees the book read-only until the edit lock is free.
- Undo history lasts until the page is closed or reloaded.
- Amounts are in dollars. There is no other currency.
- Recurring bills repeat monthly, on day 1 to 28. There are no weekly or yearly bills.
- Receipts and attachments cannot be stored.
- There is no budget or spending limit.
- The summary cannot be printed from MoneyBags; export the Excel workbook and print it there.
- Import reads the Expenses sheet of the old workbook layout only. Payments noted beside the Monthly Expenses sheet are not imported.
- Bank or credit card statements cannot be imported directly.
- Without a server, the book lives in one browser only and there is no sign-in.
- The server keeps 60 daily backups on the same disk as the book; copies elsewhere must be made separately.
- The program is laid out for a computer screen. On a narrow screen the lists scroll sideways.
