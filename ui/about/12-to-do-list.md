# To-do list

The To-do panel lists every expense that needs a look, in date order, across all years. The panel's tab shows how many expenses are listed.

## What lands there

| Item | Cause | How to clear it |
|---|---|---|
| Amount is blank | The amount is empty, for example a recurring bill with no fixed amount | Enter the amount |
| Date is missing | No date, for example a date the spreadsheet could not read | Enter the date |
| Joint or not is not set | The split is empty | Choose Y, N or P |
| Who paid is not set | No payer, for example an unknown letter in the spreadsheet | Choose who paid |
| (a reason) | Someone marked the expense as needs review, or the import flagged it | Mark reviewed, or **Done** in the panel |

One expense can have several items. Each item shows the vendor, the date and the amount.

## Links

| Link | What it does |
|---|---|
| Show | Clears the filters, opens the expense's month in the Expenses view and selects it |
| Done | Clears the needs review mark (only on needs review items) |

When nothing needs a look, the panel says so.
