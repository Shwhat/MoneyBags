# Years and months

MoneyBags works in calendar years, January to December. The period on screen is either one month or a whole year.

## The period

- The program opens on the current month.
- The Expenses, Payments, Summary and Charts views follow the period. The Recurring bills view shows every bill whatever the period.
- An expense or payment belongs to the month of its date. An expense with no date shows in every period until a date is entered.

## Moving between periods

| Action | Result |
|---|---|
| ‹ and › in the header, Previous month and Next month, PgUp and PgDn | One month back or forward; from a whole year, Next month opens January and Previous month opens December |
| This month | The current month |
| Whole year | All of the year on screen |
| Previous year and Next year, Shift+PgUp and Shift+PgDn | The same month (or the whole year) one year back or forward |
| Years panel | Click a year for the whole year, or a month to open it |

## Years panel

- Years are listed newest first: every year with an expense or payment, plus the current year. A new year appears on its own in January.
- Each year shows the total of its expenses. The ▼ and ▶ arrows open and close the list of months; open years are remembered for your account.
- Each month shows its total and the number of expenses. The current month is marked **now**. Pointing at a month shows its count and its net.
- **This month** and **Whole year** at the top of the panel do the same as the ribbon buttons.

## The spreadsheet years

The old spreadsheets ran from October to September. In MoneyBags each expense sits in the calendar month of its date, so October 2025 to September 2026 becomes October to December in 2025 and January to September in 2026.
