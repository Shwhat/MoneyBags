# Capabilities

This page lists what MoneyBags does in the current version, followed by what is planned.

## Available now

### Expenses

- Record an expense with date, vendor, category, who paid, split (Y joint, N for the other person, or P personal), amount, cash back, amount saved and notes.
- Track personal expenses (P): the payer keeps the whole cost, it counts in their spending, and it never changes the balance.
- Type expenses one after another in the entry row at the top of the list; Enter adds each one.
- Suggest vendors already used, and bring a known vendor's last category along.
- Show each expense's final total and each person's share.
- Sort by any column, search by text, and filter by category, who paid, split, and needs review.
- Select one or many rows with the mouse or keyboard; duplicate, delete or change them together.
- Mark an expense as needs review with a reason, and mark it reviewed again.

### Balances

- Work out who owes whom for any month, any year and as of today.
- Count expenses dated after today in their own month, and show the balance with and without them.
- Record settle-up payments, credits and adjustments between the two people.
- Settle the balance from the Balance panel with the amount filled in.

### Recurring bills

- Add one expense a month for each recurring bill, on a chosen day, from a first month to an optional last month.
- Add bills with a changing amount blank and list them in the To-do panel until the amount is entered.
- Pause and restart a bill.

### Summary and charts

- Show a month-by-month table for each year with joint spending, who paid it, what each person paid for the other, each person's personal spending, each person's share, settle-up entries, the net for the month and the running balance.
- Show totals by category with each person's share.
- Chart each person's share by month, spending by category and the balance at the end of each month, with a tooltip on every point and bar.

### Data

- Import the old Excel expense workbooks, skip rows already in the book and flag rows that need a look.
- Export expenses and payments to CSV, and the whole book to an Excel workbook with a summary sheet per year.
- Download a backup file and restore from it. Restore from the server's daily backups.

### Program

- Two accounts with sign-in; one person edits at a time.
- Light theme, dark theme, or the computer's setting.
- Menus, a ribbon that can be customized, dockable panels and saved workspaces.
- A command line, keyboard shortcuts for every common task, and a demo book.
- This guide, built into the program and available offline.
