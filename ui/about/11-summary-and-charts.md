# Summary and charts

The Summary view shows the numbers; the Charts view draws them. Both use the period on screen and the same calculations as the expense list; see [Splitting and balances](/about/splitting-and-balances).

## Summary view

### Tiles

| Tile | Shows |
|---|---|
| Balance today | The amount and who owes whom |
| Joint spending | Joint spending in the period, with what each person paid of it |
| (person)'s share | Each person's share in the period |
| Net for the period | The balance effect of the period alone |

### Month by month

One row per month of the year on screen, then a total row. "Carried in from" above the table is the balance at the end of the previous year. Click a month to open it. A dash means zero.

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Month | The month; the open month is highlighted |
| Joint | Joint spending (Y) |
| (first person) paid | Joint spending the first person paid |
| (second person) paid | Joint spending the second person paid |
| (first) for (second) | N expenses the first person paid for the second |
| (second) for (first) | N expenses the second person paid for the first |
| (person)'s share | Each person's share |
| Settle-up | Payments, credits and adjustments, both directions |
| Net | Who owes whom for that month alone |
| Balance | Who owes whom at the end of that month, counting everything before it |

Pointing at a column name shows its full name.

### By category

One row per category with expenses in the period, largest first: number of expenses, total, each person's share, and the share of the total as a percentage. Click a row to see those expenses.

A note below the tables shows any spending in the period with no split set; it counts for the payer and is left out of the balance.

## Charts view

| Chart | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Each person's share of spending by month | One line per person (first person blue, second person orange), from the book's first month or January through the current month |
| Spending by category | One bar per category for the period on screen, largest first, with the total at the end of each bar |
| Balance at the end of each month | One line; above zero the second person owes the first, below zero the first owes the second |

- Pointing at a month or a bar shows a tooltip with the values. On line charts a dashed line marks the month.
- The charts are drawn at the width of the work area and redrawn when it changes.
- The colors were chosen to stay apart for people with color blindness, and each line is also named, so color is never the only label. The dark theme uses its own steps of the same colors.
- The Summary view has every charted number as a table.
